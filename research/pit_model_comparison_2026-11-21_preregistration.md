Registered 2026-11-21 after the cutoff study and before fitting or evaluating any new arm. The 2016–2024 history is development evidence. This protocol authorizes no access to the reserved 2025 confirmation data.

## Hypothesis and decision

The sequence model can learn temporal interactions in the same price and volume history that a regularized linear model or boosted trees may miss. The falsifiable claim is that its probability-only portfolio improves annualized net daily-return Sharpe by at least 0.20 over each of L2 logistic regression, LightGBM, and six-month momentum. The null for each paired comparison is no positive Sharpe improvement. An economic or forecast advantage on these previously examined years is a reason for future independent research, with confirmation requiring its own authorized protocol.

## Statistical gate

- Primary economic comparison is LSTM minus each baseline in net Sharpe, using a shared 20-session moving-block bootstrap with 5,000 draws and seed 20261121. Use the same resampled dates for each arm. Report ordinary paired 95% two-sided intervals and Bonferroni-adjusted one-sided lower bounds (alpha 0.05/3). Report Deflated Sharpe with N=45 under the prior convention.
- Advance the LSTM only if all three point differences are at least +0.20, all three adjusted lower bounds exceed zero, and its Deflated Sharpe is at least 0.95. It must also retain a positive Sharpe difference versus all three baselines under both the cost stress and the top-three-trade diagnostic, each compared using the corresponding treatment for every arm. Forecast results explain the trading outcomes and cannot override a failed gate.
- If the LSTM fails, retain the simplest baseline whose results warrant further development, without declaring equivalence from inconclusive intervals. Leave the confirmation period sealed in all cases. Record protocol deviations before further analysis. No new forecasts or portfolio results were generated while writing this registration.
