# Book-equity evidence coverage study

Measure how much filing-verified preferred-stock treatment can recover beyond the 8,703 security-months with verified book-to-market in the closed fundamentals gate. Use the original 54,475 security-month roster and prior-session CRSP issuer caps. This is a data study. The failed fundamentals specification remains closed, the strategy trial count is 49, and the 2025 confirmation remains sealed.

- Freeze the original panel, historical WRDS snapshot manifest, dated issuer links, and the source and method for new filing evidence. Keep the original cutoff, vintage precedence, exact fiscal-period join, units, common equity definition, and issuer capitalization. Only the source evidence for preferred shares changes.
- For the new evidence, require original accession primary statements or equity notes to identify the parent and the balance-sheet date. Accept an explicit statement of zero preferred shares outstanding when no nonzero carrying amount, payable, redemption obligation or other conflicting preferred claim is disclosed. For a nonzero claim require a separately reported carrying value on the same accounting basis. Absence of a tag, omission from the balance sheet, authorization without an outstanding count, and narrative about a different date leave the value missing. Match the result to the dated WRDS common-equity snapshot within source rounding.
- Run a WRDS vintage integrity check on original-and-amended filings with genuinely changed common equity. Compare the first value state known after each accession and confirm the later amount is absent from earlier states, with special attention to initial loads and backfills. Nonfinancial amendments are a negative control. Quarantine ambiguous dates or values and report each discordance.
- Freeze a fresh audit from eligible newly recovered and rejected accession candidates before inspecting values. Use seed 20261123 and exclude all 160 prior audit IDs. Stratify by original or amended form, date, size and delisting where observable. Review 120 recoveries and 40 rejections if available, including explicit zero and nonzero preferred claims. Record any shortfall. Accounting concept, currency, fiscal period and timing must agree exactly. A sampled value must agree within reported rounding.
- Compare paired security-month availability against the original panel. Report recovered and lost keys, overall and yearly rates, size groups, the delisting subgroup, and missingness by cause. Report a separate theoretical ceiling that ignores preferred-stock verification and is never treated as observed coverage. Keep the 80%, 70% and 60% thresholds as reference points. No full replacement panel or predictive experiment qualifies unless the new evidence and fresh audit clear review.
