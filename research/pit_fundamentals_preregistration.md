# Point-in-time fundamentals data gate

The hypothesis is that independently filing-validated profitability, asset growth, and book-to-market can be formed for at least 80% of eligible historical S&P 500 security-months. This is a data gate. The strategy ledger stays at 49.

- Freeze the 2016–2024 XNYS month-end roster before joining fundamentals and retain historical members that later delist. Use the dated CRSP S&P 500 roster as the independent security-level denominator, with membership as of the signal session and no fundamental or price availability filter. Use pre-2016 history for fiscal comparisons. The knowledge cutoff is the December 31, 2024 market close.
- Prefer WRDS snapshots with a verified SEC accession, acceptance time, accounting concept, period, currency, basis, and dated issuer link. LSEG requires independently dated availability and identical filing evidence. Keep SEC reconstructions separate. Quarantine conflicts. Roll SEC acceptances after 17:30 Eastern to the following date. Treat vendor date-only records as end of day. Select the first session open strictly after both availability times and apply the prior-session close cutoff. Keep original values until a verified amendment becomes usable.
- Calculate quarterly net income over positive assets, year-over-year asset growth against the latest known comparable prior-year quarter, and parent common book equity over prior-session issuer market cap. Derive Q4 income from compatible annual and nine-month inputs. Aggregate verified common classes by PERMCO. Flag negative equity and leave unresolved classes, fiscal transitions, and incompatible bases missing.
- Require all three features for at least 80% of the frozen roster pooled, 70% per year, and 60% per prior-session size tercile and in the subsequent 12-month delisting subgroup. Use only fully observable windows for the delisting subgroup and report excluded counts. Report missingness by month, source, and reason.
- Freeze the stratified audit before inspecting sampled values, using seed 20261121. Audit 120 accepted and 40 rejected issuer-quarter vintages, with dedicated original, amendment, and delisted reference cases. Reconcile identity, concept, period, currency and basis exactly, value within filing rounding, and independently derived issuer market cap within 1%. Any unresolved discrepancy blocks clearance. Report coverage with and without LSEG and require an extra 30 LSEG vintages with zero failures if LSEG determines a pass.
- Claude reviews source and join logic, clears the full coverage audit, then reviews the decision. Preserve licensed rows locally. A failed gate closes this specification. A source-access blocker leaves coverage unmeasured and the panel unapproved. This gate uses no strategy returns, labels, or confirmation-period data.
